### dynamic power
$$
P_{\text{dynamic}}=\frac{1}{2}CV^{2}f
$$

Capacitance $C$ is impacted by the complexity of the circuits.
### static power
$$
P_{\text{static}}=V_{\text{supply}}I_{\text{leakage}}
$$
## Energy Consumption

$$
E=\int P(t)dt
$$
$P(t)$ gives instantaneous power at a given time.
It's important that $P(t)$ does not exceed $P_{max}$ which is also known as TDP (max designed power)
