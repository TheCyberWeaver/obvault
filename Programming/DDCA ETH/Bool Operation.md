## Normal Forms
Note that AND is represented as Sum, OR is represented as Product.
The reason for this deep connection is that $\{ 0,1 \}$ forms a semiring under the bool operation, where AND acts like $\otimes$ and OR acts like $\oplus$. 
- Product-of-Sums Form $\simeq$ CNF ([[Propositional Logic#CNF & DNF|Conjunctive Normal Form]])
- Sum of Products Form $\simeq$ DNF ([[Propositional Logic#CNF & DNF|Disjunctive Normal Form]])
