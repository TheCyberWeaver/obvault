# [[Kernel]]
For a linear transformation $T$
$\mathrm{Ker}(T):=\{\mathbf{x}\in \mathbb{R}^{n}:T(x)=\mathbf{0}\}\subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n}$
$$
\mathrm{Ker}(T)=\mathrm{N}(A)
$$

> [!NOTE]
> The kernel of a matrix is the null space of it

# Image
For a linear transformation $T$
$\mathrm{Im}(T):=\{T(\mathbf{x}):\mathbf{x}\in \mathbb{R}^{n}\}\subseteq \mathbb{R}^{m}$

In matrix 
$$
\mathrm{Im}(T)=\mathrm{C}(A)
$$
