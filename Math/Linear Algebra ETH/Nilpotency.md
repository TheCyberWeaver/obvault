## Definition
A square matrix $A$ is nilpotent iff there exists an integer $k>1$ $s$.$t$. $A^{k}=0$.
The smallest such $k$ is called the index of nilpotency.

## Equivalent Characterizations
- All [[Eigenvalues|eigenvalues]] are $0$ (over $\mathbb{C}$ or over $\mathbb{R}$)
- The characteristic polynomial is $P_{A}(\lambda)=\lambda ^{n}$
- $A$ is similar to a direct sum of Jordan blocks with eigenvalues $0$


## Useful fact
if $A$ is $n\times n$ nilpotent, then:
- $A^{n}=0$
- $Tr(A^{m})=0$ for all $m\geq1$
