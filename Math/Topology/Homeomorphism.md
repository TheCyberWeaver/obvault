## relation to category theory
A homeomorphism is an isomorphism in the [[Category Theory Intro|category]] of topological spaces. Therefore, the composition of two homeomorphisms is also a homeomorphism, and all self-homeomorphisms $X \to X$ form a group, called the homeomorphism group of $X$, usually denoted by $Homeo(X)$.
