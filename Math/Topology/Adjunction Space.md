Let $X$ and $Y$ be spaces, $A\subseteq Y$ closed, and $f:A\to X$ a continuous function.
The _adjunction space_ is defined as $X\cup _{f}Y:=X\sqcup Y/\sim$
where $a\sim f(a)$, for all $a\in A$

## Example: Attaching manifolds along boundary

![[Pasted image 20260222233944.png]]

$X$ and $Y$ are n-dimensional [[Manifold#manifold with boundary (not a manifold)|manifold with boundary]]

This gives a natural way to construct a [[Manifold|manifold]] from manifolds with boundary

### Special case
If $X=Y$, then $X\cup _{\partial X}X$ is called the "double" of $X$.
