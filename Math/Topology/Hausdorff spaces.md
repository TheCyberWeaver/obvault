## Definition
A topological space $X$ is called **Hausdorff** if any pair of distinct points have disjoint neighborhoods
![[Pasted image 20251211225557.png|297]]

### Example
Every metric space is Hausdorff

## Proposition
- Any finite subset of a Hausdorff space is closed.
- Convergent sequences have unique limits (main goal why we define Hausdorff space)
- If $p \in X$ is a limit point of $A$, then any neighborhood of $p$ contains infinitely many points of $A$



> [!NOTE] 
> Every metric space is Hausdorff. (the converse is false)
