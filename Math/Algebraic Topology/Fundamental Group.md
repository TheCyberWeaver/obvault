## Definition
If the starting and ending point of a [[Path Connectedness|path]] are the same, then this path is called a **loop**
The common starting and ending point $x_{0}$ is referred to as the **basepoint**

The set of all [[Homotopy#Homotopy Class|homotopy classes]] $[f]$ of loops $f:I\to X$ at the basepoint $x_{0}$ is denoted $\pi_{1}(X,x_{0})$

> [!NOTE] Proposition 1.3 ([[Algebraic Topology.pdf#page=35&selection=603,0,604,14&color=yellow|Algebraic Topology, p.35]])
> $\pi_{1}(X,x_{0})$ is a group with respect to the product $[f][g]=[f\centerdot g]$ 

This group is called the **fundamental group** of $X$ at the basepoint $x_{0}$

Further we can show that the map $\beta _{h} : \pi_{1}(X,x_{1})\to \pi_{1}(X,x_{0})$ is an isomorphism

Thus if $X$ is [[Path Connectedness|path-connected]], the group $\pi_1(X, x_0)$ is, up to isomorphism, independent of the choice of basepoint $x_0$. In this case the notation $\pi_1(X, x_0)$ is often abbreviated to $\pi_1(X)$ or $\pi_1 X$.
