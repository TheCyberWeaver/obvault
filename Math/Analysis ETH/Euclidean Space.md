## Standard Inner product
$$
<x,y> := \sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}y_{i}
$$
## Norm
$$
\lVert x \rVert:=\sqrt{ \sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}^{2} }
$$
## Distance
The canonical [[Metric|metric]] on $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ is the Euclidean distance
$$
d(x,y):=\lVert x-y \rVert_{2}=\sqrt{ \sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_{i}-y_{i})^{2} }
$$
we can check the axioms of a metric (see [[Metric#Axioms]])
