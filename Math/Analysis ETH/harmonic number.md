## Definition

The $n$-th harmonic number is:
$$
H_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{k}
$$
## Useful Bounds
$$
\ln(n+1) \le H_n \le \ln(n) + 1
$$
## Estimation
For large $n$:
$$
H_n \approx \ln(n) + \gamma
$$
- $\ln(n)$: natural logarithm  
- $\gamma$: Euler–Mascheroni constant $\approx 0.57721$

**Refined Estimate**
$$
H_n = \ln(n) + \gamma + \frac{1}{2n} - O\!\left(\frac{1}{n^2}\right)
$$
