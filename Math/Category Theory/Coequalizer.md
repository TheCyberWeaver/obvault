# Definition
- Given a morphism $f: A \to B$, the **kernel pair** $A \times_B A \rightrightarrows A$ defines an [[Relation#Equivalence Relations|equivalence relation]] (a _congruence_ in algebraic categories).
- The quotient $A/\sim$ is the **coequalizer** of those two morphisms.

$$
A \times_B A \rightrightarrows A\to A /\sim 
$$
### Example
- $G\to G / N$ in **Grp** (see [[Section 14 Factor Groups]])
- $R\to R / I$ in **Ring** (see [[Section 26 Homomorphisms and Factor Rings]])
- $S\to S / \sim$ in **Set** (see [[Relation#Equivalence Relations]])
