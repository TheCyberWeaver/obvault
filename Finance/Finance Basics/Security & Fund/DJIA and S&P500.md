The **DJIA** and **S&P 500** are major U.S. stock market indexes, but they are built differently.

| Aspect | DJIA | S&P 500 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Number of companies | 30 | 500 |
| Weighting | Price-weighted | Market-cap-weighted |
| Coverage | Narrower, blue-chip focus | Broader large-cap market |

Why the weighting matters:

- In the **DJIA**, a higher stock price has more influence on the index.
- In the **S&P 500**, a larger company has more influence because weighting depends on market value.

The S&P 500 is usually the better broad benchmark for the U.S. large-cap equity market.
