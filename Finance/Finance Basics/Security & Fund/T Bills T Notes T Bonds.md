U.S. Treasury securities mainly differ by maturity.

| Security | Maturity | Cash flow pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **T-bills** | 1 year or less | Sold at a discount; no coupon |
| **T-notes** | 2 to 10 years | Semiannual coupon payments |
| **T-bonds** | 20 to 30 years | Semiannual coupon payments |

All three are generally viewed as very low-credit-risk instruments because they are backed by the U.S. government.

Longer maturity usually means greater sensitivity to interest-rate changes, so T-bonds typically carry more price risk than T-bills.
