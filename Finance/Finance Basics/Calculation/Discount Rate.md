The **discount rate** is the rate of return used to convert future cash flows into present value.

It usually reflects some combination of:

- the time value of money
- expected inflation
- risk

Higher discount rate -> lower present value.  
Lower discount rate -> higher present value.

This concept is essential for:

- [[PV & NPV]]
- [[Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)|DCF]]
- [[Time Value of Money]]
