### FRED

**FRED** stands for **Federal Reserve Economic Data**. It is a widely used source for macroeconomic and financial time series.

Typical categories include:

- interest rates and credit markets
- inflation and prices
- GDP and national accounts
- employment and labor markets
- international and regional data

It is useful when you need time-series data for notes such as [[GDP (Gross Domestic Product)|GDP]], [[Unemployment]], or [[Inflation Index]].
