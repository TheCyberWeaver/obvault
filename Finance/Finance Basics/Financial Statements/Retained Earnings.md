**Retained earnings** are the cumulative profits kept in the business rather than paid out as dividends.

Formula:

$$\text{Ending Retained Earnings}=\text{Beginning Retained Earnings}+\text{Net Income}-\text{Dividends}$$

Retained earnings increase with profit and decrease with dividend payments.

They are part of [[Shareholder's equity]] and connect the [[Income Statement]] to the balance sheet.
