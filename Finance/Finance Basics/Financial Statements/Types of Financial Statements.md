The four core financial statements answer different questions about the same business.

## 1. [[Income Statement]]

Shows profitability over a period.

## 2. [[Cash Flow statement|Cash Flow Statement]]

Shows where cash came from and where it went during a period.

## 3. [[Balance Sheet]]

Shows assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time.

## 4. [[Shareholder's equity|Statement of Shareholders' Equity]]

Shows how owners’ equity changed during a period.

Best way to read them:

- use the income statement for profit
- use the cash flow statement for cash reality
- use the balance sheet for financial position
- use the equity statement for ownership changes
