**Common-size statements** restate financial statements in percentage terms so companies can be compared more easily.

Common forms:

- **Common-size income statement**: each line shown as a percentage of revenue
- **Common-size balance sheet**: each line shown as a percentage of total assets

Why useful:

- makes firms of different sizes easier to compare
- highlights margin structure and asset mix
- helps spot trend changes over time

This is one of the simplest tools for comparing firms after understanding the basic [[Types of Financial Statements]].
