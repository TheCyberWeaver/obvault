Financial reporting breaks business activity into defined **accounting periods** so performance can be measured consistently.

Common periods:

- **monthly**
- **quarterly**
- **annually**

Why this matters:

- investors can compare results over time
- revenue and expenses are matched to the correct period
- it supports the periodic reporting required by [[GAAP]]

This idea is closely tied to [[Accrual-Basis Accounting & Cash-Basis Accounting]].
