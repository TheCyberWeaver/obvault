The **income statement** shows profitability over a period.

Basic flow:

$$\text{Revenue} - \text{Expenses} = \text{Net Income}$$

Common lines:

- revenue
- cost of goods sold
- operating expenses
- interest expense
- tax expense
- net income

What it answers:

- Did the company make money during the period?
- Where are the main costs?
- Are margins improving or weakening?

One widely used derived metric is [[EBITDA]], but it should not replace full statement analysis.
