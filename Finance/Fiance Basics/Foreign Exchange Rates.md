# How Exchange Rates Interact with National Economic Policies

**1. Monetary Policy 货币政策**  
When a central bank (like the Federal Reserve or People’s Bank of China) changes interest rates, it influences the exchange rate:
- **Higher interest rates** attract foreign capital → **currency appreciates (升值)**.
- **Lower interest rates** push capital out → **currency depreciates (贬值)**.

**2. Fiscal Policy 财政政策**  
Government spending and taxation also affect exchange rates.
- A government that **increases spending** or **cuts taxes** may boost the economy → possibly leading to **currency appreciation** if investors believe it will lead to growth.
- But if spending causes **budget deficits (预算赤字)**, it might lead to **depreciation**, especially if investors lose confidence.

**3. Trade Policy 贸易政策**  
Tariffs, quotas, and subsidies affect the **trade balance (贸易平衡)**:
- A **trade surplus (顺差)** supports a stronger currency. 
	- Export>Import -> 外国买家对于本国货币需求增加 -> 升值
- A **trade deficit (逆差)** can weaken the currency.
	- Import>Export -> 外国买家对于本国货币需求减少 -> 贬值

**4. Currency Intervention 汇率干预**  
Governments or central banks may **buy or sell their own currency** to influence its value:
- Buying own currency → **appreciation**
- Selling own currency → **depreciation**

### Exchange Rates -> Economy
-  Appreciation makes exports more expensive and imports cheaper, which can over time reduce a surplus.    
- Depreciation makes exports cheaper and imports costlier, which can help correct a deficit by boosting export volumes.


> [!Tip]
> **政策利率（Policy Rate，政策利率）** is NOT “存款利率”或“贷款利率”
### 1. 政策利率（Policy Rate，政策利率）

- **含义**：中央银行向商业银行借出或借入资金所使用的基准利率，是影响全市场利率的核心工具。
    
- **结构**：
    - **回购利率（Repo Rate，回购利率）／再贷款利率（Refinancing Rate，重融资利率）**：中央银行向银行提供短期资金的利率，相当于“借钱的成本”。
        
    - **存款便利利率（Deposit Facility Rate，存款便利利率）／超额准备金利率（Excess Reserve Rate）**：商业银行将多余资金存放在中央银行可获得的利率，相当于“存钱的收益”。
        

> **举例（美国）**
> 
> - **联邦基金利率（Federal Funds Rate，联邦基金利率）**：美国联储系统调控的银行间隔夜拆借利率，核心上类似于回购利率，影响市场上存贷款利率。
>     
> - 中央银行并不直接设定家户存款的利率或企业贷款利率，却通过改变以上政策利率，间接影响商业银行给客户的存贷款利率。

# Exchange Rate Risk

| Aspect          | Translation Exposure (会计风险)                               | Economic Exposure (经济风险)                                      |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Impact on**   | Financial statements                                      | Long-term cash flows and market value                         |
| **Nature**      | Accounting (book value fluctuations)                      | Operational (real cash flow changes)                          |
| **Time Frame**  | Short-term, immediate accounting periods                  | Long-term, affecting future performance                       |
| **Affected By** | Exchange rate changes on financial statements             | Exchange rate changes on costs, revenues, and competitiveness |
| **Example**     | Currency changes affecting the consolidated balance sheet | Currency changes affecting future sales abroad                |
| **Risk Type**   | Accounting/Reporting Risk                                 | Operational/Strategic Risk                                    |
## Summary

- **Translation exposure** is a reporting issue that affects the company’s financial statements but not actual cash flows.
- **Economic exposure** affects the actual economic value of the company and its future cash flows, influencing competitive strategy and overall profitability.

## Example

|操作场景|属于哪种风险|理由说明|
|---|---|---|
|你将日本子公司的报表折算成美元|会计风险（Translation）|只是汇率换算，账面数据变了，但运营没变。|
|你从日本采购原材料，日元升值了|经济风险（Economic）|成本上升，利润减少，影响了实际现金流和盈利能力。|
|你在欧洲有大量销售，欧元贬值了|经济风险（Economic）|收入减少，实际影响公司未来现金流。|
|会计上显示资产缩水但无实际亏损|会计风险（Translation）|只是报表上看资产变少，实际没损失现金。|
